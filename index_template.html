How minting works on OpenSea

Clear, straightforward facts. No hidden contract conditions, no wallet connections on this page.

Where does minting happen?

Strictly on OpenSea. There is zero payment flow on this website. You will never be asked to connect a MetaMask wallet or approve a transaction here. We only record your Ethereum wallet address from the whitelist memory game to authorize your mint spot.

⚓ OpenSea Verified Contract ERC-721

What are the supply and mint details?

The collection consists of 5,555 unique portraits on the Ethereum network. Mint price is 0.008 ETH (accessible and friendly, about the price of two cups of coffee with a fren).

What is the difference between GTD and FCFS?

GTD (Guaranteed): Players who solved the board in ≤ 30.0s receive a dedicated 12-hour reserved window where their mint is 100% secured.

FCFS: Players who solved the board in 30.1s – 59.9s can mint during the second tier window on OpenSea.

Can I retry the memory game?

If you take 60 seconds or more, your 30-minute cooldown timer begins. Once the timer reaches 00:00, you can jump back in. If you achieved an FCFS spot and want to strive for GTD, you can always play again using your handle.
